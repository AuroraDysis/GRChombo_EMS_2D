# T17 registered merger design — for the operator's admission decision

This is a design and bounded local qualification, not a merger run. The executable baseline is fork main **77c5b6f**, plus the uncommitted [production patch](t17-production.patch). The patch adds opt-in two-centre refinement and uses the existing puncture tracker; the native gauge, KO, field equations, point transfers, RHFinder, Float64 and puncture treatment are unchanged. `ems_binary_refinement` and `ems_track_punctures` default off. The operator's exact alpha=0.9, e=8 profile and documented-diagnostic n32-r6 CTT companion are SHA-256 pinned in [inputs](t17-inputs.json). The companion's C1 interface defect remains; this design does not admit a convergence campaign.

The run-1 reading is the notebook's 2026-10-01T17:55Z / 2026-10-02T01:10Z decision: **measure losses** from the tuned zero-loss configuration. Its terminal e need not equal seven; positive radiative loss moves it upward. Measure GW, EM and scalar channels, including scalar monopole and the early launch. Use the result to retune a later run, whose target acceptance is seven +/-0.5. If run 1 already satisfies that interval it counts. Do not discard early emission as junk when forming its loss budget.

## Domain, duration and restart

The outer radius is **2240 M_i**. With a registered envelope V<=2, a boundary disturbance present at t=0 cannot reach r=100 before **1070 M_i**. For an outward launch starting as far as the initial puncture at |x|=16, its reflection cannot return before 4364 M_i at light speed or 2182 M_i at V=2. The stronger one-way condition covers initial outer-boundary mismatch. Actual initial and short-window maxima are measured; the future merger needs a write-only characteristic-speed monitor. The envelope is a monitored assumption, not a nonlinear theorem or a prediction of its long-run maximum. A measured violation or a duration beyond the travel budget invalidates the registered domain criterion.

The exact principal-block/travel-time witnesses, assumptions and independent numerical eigenvalue checks are in [budget](t17-budget.json). They use the actual hard-coded lapse coefficient 1.8, not the ignored lapse_coeff=2 parameter. They prove only the stated scalar-lapse and longitudinal-shift blocks. The fastest directional speed census includes |beta|+sqrt(lambda_max(h_inverse)), |beta|+alpha sqrt(chi lambda_max), and |beta|+sqrt(1.8 alpha chi lambda_max), with hww included.

Use the supplied initial M_ADM=2.002174604 M_i as **M_ref**, an upper clock for a positive-loss final mass. Run 1 goes through the first **detected, qualified common horizon**, then at least 100 M_ref. At the 306-M_i leading-order infall estimate this is 506.2174604 M_i, rounded to **1158 coarse steps / 506.625 M_i**. The stop time in the parameter file is a planning value: replace it from the actual first-common detection clock. A fixed estimated stop must not certify 100 M_f if the merger happens later. The clock based on initial mass avoids feeding a later static model into evolution.

Checkpoint continuation through 200/300 M_ref gives 706.4349208/906.6523812 M_i, or **1615/2073 coarse steps**, still inside the one-way budget with margins 363.4375/163.0625 M_i after rounding. The larger domain is chosen initially; an ordinary restart cannot enlarge its domain. At 100 M_ref inspect the final 50-M_f plateau (delta e_H<0.1), flux histories, charge and exterior constraints before deciding whether to continue. Neither an exit zero at a segment boundary nor reaching the estimated infall time means run 1 is complete.

## Horizon schedule and cost model

The draft disables live RH calls; the author currently updates configured surfaces on every matching level callback. `RH_time_step_freq` cannot implement the intended inexpensive cadence. A bounded **checkpoint probe driver** supplies cadence and fresh common-search lifecycle without modifying RHUnion/RHSurf. Individual qualified 48/96 histories begin at t=0, then every 25 M_i through the common horizon (planning 13 clocks x 2 surfaces =26 qualified pairs). A cheap N48 warm history at every eighth coarse checkpoint, 3.5 M_i, retains intermediate drift; its cost needs the measured per-update timing and must be recorded separately.

Start fresh common searches at t>=250 M_i or tracker separation<=1 M_i, whichever comes first; probe every eighth coarse step (3.5 M_i). Predeclare spherical seeds r=max(2R_h,1.05d(t)/2) and 2r, centred at the binary midpoint, each capped at 240 s at N48. A failed or capped search is not proof that no common horizon exists and does not permanently kill later independent probes. Qualify a found common surface with N48/96 and all three residual stages at that same checkpoint. After first qualified detection, retain common N48 history every 3.5 M_i and strict 48/96 pairs every 5 M_ref, including the 100-M_ref endpoint (planning 21 qualified pairs). Stop and classify “common horizon not demonstrated” if none qualifies by 350 M_i; the infall estimate is not substituted for a horizon. Additional angular convergence needed for questionable surfaces is an admission decision, not an automatic operator variant.

The user-supplied exp-0023 cost is **2006–3719 s per three-stage N48/96 probe** at max level 14. Conservatively pricing 47 qualified surface pairs gives **26.19–48.55 node-hours**, plus bounded cold common searches (29 scheduled clocks x two seeds x 240 s =3.87 h over the 250–350-M_i window), warm histories and snapshot I/O. An earlier separation-triggered start has an absolute bound of 100 eighth-step clocks through 350 M_i, or 13.33 h for two capped seeds at each clock; actual cost must retain both clocks and attempted seeds. The local two-rung probes measure actual update/total times before final admission. Cluster caps must permit the supplied multi-thousand-second costs; the existing local diagnostic harness's 1795-s per-N limit is not an automatically sufficient cluster cap. Increasing the wrapper's bound changes no finder algorithm.

Let b be the measured binary/single coarse-step median ratio on this machine and w the actual chosen single-hole cell-step work relative to exp-0020's E-low initial boxes (sum cells_l 2^l). The 13-level anchor gives **256 b w s/step**. Normalizing the 15-level **1030 s/step** anchor by the previously measured extra-source work factor 3.997438712 gives **257.665 b w s/step**. Run-1 evolution alone is therefore about **82.35–82.88 b w node-hours** before the planning factor 0.5–2. The actual b,w and selected rung are pending; no measured binary rate is invented. One node means 32 MPI x4 OpenMP, 128 allocated cores. Node-hours equal wall-hours on that node, not core-hours.

Use 12-h leaves with at most 10 h planned evolution, conservative 2x step cost, checkpoint/exit handling in the reserved time, and qualified finder work in separately bounded leaves. Round the per-leaf coarse-step quota down to an eight-step boundary. If fewer than eight steps fit, use single-step checkpoints and that smaller quota; if no full coarse step fits, the 12-hour plan fails admission. For the illustration b=2,w=1 this is 32 coarse steps per leaf, about 37 evolution leaves for 1158 steps; it is **not a measured estimate**. The draft max_steps=32 is the first-leaf limit; each restart raises the absolute step limit. Preserve every eighth-step checkpoint until its probes/reductions pass; retain t0, qualified history clocks, endpoint and two rolling restart points. Disable large plot output. Plan scratch from the actual checkpoint census (likely tens to hundreds of GB with selected checkpoints), never the local 16-GB evidence ceiling. A segment exit produces segment.done, not run1.done; run1.done requires actual first-common+100-M_ref coverage and complete diagnostics or an explicit classified abort.
